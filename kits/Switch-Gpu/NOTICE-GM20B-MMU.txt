Linux nouveau v6.12, nvkm/subdev/mmu/vmmgf100.c, gf100_vmm_pgd_pde.

The per-instance PDB big-page selection follows gm20b_fb_set_mmu_page_size
in Switchroot nvgpu common/fb/fb_gm20b.c (MIT), with the field definition in
include/nvgpu/hw/gm20b/hw_fb_gm20b.h. Nouveau v6.12 subdev/fb/gm200.c,
gm200_fb_init_page, was consulted for the equivalent per-instance mode.

The GM20B bit-34 device-address tag follows Linux nouveau v6.12:
nouveau_platform.c (gm20b_platform_data), Copyright 2014 NVIDIA CORPORATION;
nvkm/subdev/instmem/gk20a.c (gk20a_instobj_ctor_iommu), Copyright 2015 NVIDIA
CORPORATION. These upstream files carry the MIT permission below.
https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/gpu/drm/nouveau/nouveau_platform.c
https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.12/drivers/gpu/drm/nouveau/nvkm/subdev/instmem/gk20a.c

The SoC DRAM table-target/VOL policy is adapted from the MIT-licensed
mesa-switch-woa core/gm20b_mmu.c and platform/t214_mmu.c, which cite
Switchroot nvgpu common/fb/fb_gm20b.c. Only this MMU policy is used here;
the 128 KiB big-page geometry is retained from the existing probe.
https://github.com/ruje0504-2/mesa-switch-woa/blob/main/tegra-x1-wddm/core/gm20b_mmu.c
https://github.com/ruje0504-2/mesa-switch-woa/blob/main/tegra-x1-wddm/platform/t214_mmu.c
